UCU LOST AND FOUND - FRONTEND (READY TO RUN)

1. Right-click the ZIP file -> Extract All. Move the extracted folder to a simple place such as C:\Projects
   (avoid OneDrive/Desktop folders, they slow down installs).
2. Open the folder and DOUBLE-CLICK  START-DEMO.bat
   (If Windows warns you, click "More info" -> "Run anyway".)
3. Wait 1-3 minutes the first time. Your browser opens by itself.
